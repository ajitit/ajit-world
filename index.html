AI & Technology Vision

Where Enterprise Technology Is Going

AI-first → AI-native

Adding AI features to existing systems is the transitional state. The end state is AI-native: platforms designed around agents, retrieval and human-AI workflows from the first architectural decision. I build and operate this pattern today.

The human + AI workforce

Agents will take structured work across the SDLC and business processes; people move to intent, judgement and exception handling. The operating model — HITL gates, accountability, skills — is the hard part, and it is a leadership problem, not a tooling one.

Governance is the enabler

In regulated industries, auditability is not friction — it is the licence to deploy. Audit-by-design, append-only history and policy-as-code let AI move fast because the controls are structural, not procedural.

Data + AI convergence

Every AI outcome is downstream of data quality. Golden data, ontologies and knowledge graphs — once “governance projects” — are now the retrieval substrate of enterprise AI. The decade I spent on FAIR data is why the AI chapter works.

AI-forward engineering

Agentic SDLC — agents for requirements, code, test, deployment paired with engineers — changes team shape and economics. Leaders should pilot it inside a governed delivery flow, measure, then scale what proves out.

Platforms over projects

Reusable platform capability — gateway, retrieval, evaluation, observability — compounds; one-off AI projects don’t. Enterprise AI investment should build the platform once and let every use case ride it.
